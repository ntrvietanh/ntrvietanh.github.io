Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Verrata Studio Publisher ("the tool") is operated by Verrata Studio and is used only by the studio to publish its own content to its own TikTok account.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission granted through TikTok Login Kit, the tool accesses the account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and creator posting settings, and uploads and publishes videos to that account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). The tool does not collect any personal data of viewers or other TikTok users.

Storage and sharing

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored privately in the studio's own secured work environment and are never published. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with any third party.

Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept only while the studio uses the tool. The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions; the tool then deletes the stored tokens.

Related policies

Use of TikTok services is also governed by the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.
