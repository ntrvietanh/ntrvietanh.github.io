Verrata Studio Publisher

Verrata Studio Publisher is the in-house publishing tool of Verrata Studio, a Vietnamese science and history explainer studio (@verratastudio on TikTok). The studio uses it to upload its own short videos, with their captions, to its own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works: the account owner signs in once with Login Kit and grants permission. When a short is ready, the tool reads the account's creator info, lets the owner choose the privacy level and interaction settings, uploads the video file, and posts it to the owner's account. Nothing is posted without the owner's choice.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It only ever acts on accounts owned by Verrata Studio.
